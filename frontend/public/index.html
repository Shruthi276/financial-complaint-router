How to use the Complaint Router

  1. Paste the complaint. Copy the customer's message into the big box on the Route a complaint tab. It must be at least 20 characters. Or click one of the examples.
  2. Click “Route complaint”. After about a second you will see the result.
  3. Read the result:
    • Routed to: the team that should handle the complaint.
    • Priority: Urgent complaints (fraud, foreclosure, threats, inability to pay…) should be handled first.
    • Confidence: how sure the system is (High, Medium or Low). Low means below 50%. Please check it yourself.
    • Next step: what to do, in one sentence: Handle first, Check before assigning or Ready to assign.
    • Words that drove the decision and similar past complaints explain why.
  4. Tell us if it was right. Click Yes, correct, or choose the right team and click Send correction. Corrections are stored encrypted and used to retrain the model.

Troubleshooting

You seeWhat to do
Red dot “Model offline”The model is starting or has not been trained yet. Wait a minute. An administrator can run make train.
“Please write a bit more”The text is too short to judge. Add more detail about the problem.
Confidence “Low, needs review”The system is unsure. Read the complaint and pick the team yourself, then send the correction.
“The text was changed”You edited the complaint after routing. Click Route complaint again.

For administrators

The Pipeline console shows each pipeline stage, its runs and failures, and the model versions. Retrain now needs the admin token from the .env file. The Monitoring tab shows latency, prediction mix and data drift. When drift lasts more than a minute, retraining starts automatically.

The full manual is in docs/user_manual.md.
